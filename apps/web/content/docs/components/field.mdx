Field and its related parts are included in the Form registry item.

## Usage

Use FieldLabel, FieldDescription and FieldError around your form controls. Import these parts from `@/components/ui/field` after installing Form.

## Composition

Field layout is a web adaptation composed from Minecraft text. It has no independent native Minecraft surface. Pair it with a [Button](/docs/components/button) when submitting a form.
